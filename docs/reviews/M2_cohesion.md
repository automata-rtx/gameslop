# M2 cohesion review (M2.17)

**Reviewer:** design-reviewer (15 §1). **Date:** 2026-10-09. **Scope:** `main` at `7af43bc` (M2.1 to M2.16, R12, R13, the Relief retreat, R14/R14b). **Against:** the pillars (00 §3), 01 to 13, the Interfaces contracts, the Glossary and every CHANGELOG ruling of the build.

Findings are ranked by how much they damage the identity line, *how real the world looks is how alive you are*: first what breaks a rule the player is taught (pillar 2) or a promise printed on screen, then what weakens the world's reality or its legibility, then housekeeping. Each finding has evidence (`file:line`) and a concrete fix. Nothing in game code was changed by this review.

## Blocking

### B1. The Lightbearer's crank ×1.5 is never applied, but its card promises it (cp-11)
The loadout card on the title says "Cranks 1.5 times faster" (`game/src/core/strings.gd:293`, 05 §7). `GameState.start_run` copies `crank_rate_mult` into `RunState` (`game/src/core/game_state.gd:50`), but nothing reads it: `Flashlight.lightbearer` (`game/src/player/flashlight.gd:38`) is declared, used by `step_charge` (`flashlight.gd:90-92`, `:172`) and never set anywhere in `game/src`. A Lightbearer player cranks at 25/s and is told otherwise; the loadout keeps only its cost (no Polaroid, Flicker attaches from 6 m). This breaks 01 §6 rule 5 ("hints must be actionable and true") on a cp-11 deliverable, and it is the open item "apply `RunState.crank_rate_mult`" (`docs/qa/open_items.md:32`) that M2.10 left behind.
**Fix:** in the run's player setup (`Player.reset_for_run` or `Run._arrive`), set the flashlight's rate from the run: replace the `lightbearer: bool` with `rate_mult: float = GameState.run.crank_rate_mult` (or set `lightbearer = run.crank_rate_mult > 1.0`), and add a test that a Lightbearer run cranks 0 to 100 in 2.67 s, a Faller in 4 s.

## Should-fix

Ranked from most to least damage.

### S1. The fuse cannot be pulled after the breaker is thrown: the Offices light dilemma lost its loop (ruling outside 08's intent)
08 §5 (`docs/design/08_entities.md:73`) makes the Variant B fuse the player's switch between "light and a working exit" and "dark and a dead one": "pulling the fuse again unpowers the floor but seals the exit; a loop the player can exploit at the cost of two 25 m noises". `Breaker.pull_fuse` refuses once the lever is thrown (`game/src/interactables/breaker.gd:110-111`, also `:125`, `:135`), recorded as an M2.8 reading (`docs/design/CHANGELOG.md:115`). 09 is silent on it, so 08 (lower number) wins and the reading narrowed the design. This is the counters-in-conflict lever M3.4 is told to verify ("the Still/Flicker light dilemma verified in Offices", 15 §2 M3): without it a thrown breaker is permanent and the Offices choice is made once, not managed.
**Fix:** after the lever is thrown, keep offering `[HOLD E] PULL FUSE` (0.8 s); pulling unpowers every group the wave powered (`LightPool.set_all_powered(false)` minus the spawn lobby group that never starts dark) and seals the exit (`Exit.seal()`, status back to `EXIT: POWERED`); inserting it again re-runs `throw_breaker` (lever, 25 m `mech`, the wave). Amend the CHANGELOG line 115 reading. Tests: insert, throw, pull, re-insert in an Offices Variant B level; Flicker's habitat follows power state.

### S2. Flicker intensity at 0.3 still flickers at 8 to 20 Hz (accessibility, ruling outside 12's intent)
12 §6 (`docs/design/12_settings_and_accessibility.md:77`) promises "Flicker's tell remains visible at 0.3 as a slow pulse 2 Hz". The build only scales the depth of each off instant (`game/src/lighting/fixture.gd:245-249`), and the CHANGELOG records "its 2 Hz slow pulse at 0.3 is not built" (`CHANGELOG.md:126`). 8 to 20 Hz sits in the photosensitive band; a player who turns the option down to protect themselves still gets the fast stutter, only shallower. The human check also expects the 2 Hz pulse (`docs/qa/human_check_M2.md:218-219`) and will fail.
**Fix:** below 1.0, blend the stutter toward a 2 Hz sine pulse: at 0.3 the group pulses at 2 Hz with depth 0.3; between 0.3 and 1.0 interpolate rate and depth (the Flicker's own charge-driven rate only applies at 1.0). The flashlight's attached stutter (`FlickerPresent`) follows the same setting. Keep the tell honest: the pulse only ever runs on Flicker's group (02 §6).

### S3. Echo treats every `mech` noise as a lure: a second rule (08 §1)
`ErrorEcho.LURE_KINDS` includes `mech` (`game/src/errors/error_echo.gd:21-23`) because "the radio emits `mech` (09)". Since M2.8 the radio emits its own kind `radio` (`game/src/items/items/radio_item.gd:186`, `radio_pickup.gd:57`), so the `mech` entry now only adds lures 08 §6 never lists (`08_entities.md:82`: `impact` and `radio`): the crank (12 m, at the player), the flashlight toggle (2 m), the breaker (25 m) and the vending machine (8 m). Concretely, a player who obeys the counter (stops) and then cranks or toggles the light pulls a frozen Echo off its trail to a stand-off 2 m from them (`error_echo.gd:150-158`, `echo_walk.gd:65-68`, `echo_nav.gd:58-71`): the stop no longer holds Echo where it froze. No contact results (the stand-off keeps 2 m), but the counter is no longer "stop, and it stops".
**Fix:** `LURE_KINDS = [NOISE_KIND_IMPACT, NOISE_KIND_RADIO]`; loud `mech` noises keep acting through suspicion only (Search toward them from Wander, 08 §2), never as a Follow lure. Update the M2.4 note in 08 Interfaces additions (`08_entities.md:135`) and 09 §2's radio row (`09_items_and_interactables.md:24`, "Emits `mech`") to `radio`. Test: Echo frozen behind a stopped player stays put while the player cranks for 5 s.

### S4. Cycle 2's Substrate ambient drain does not exist (pillar 1)
06 §9 lists "Cycle 2 ambient drain 0.2 per second in the Substrate only" with its own death cause and summary line (`DISSOLVED BY THE SUBSTRATE`). `Tuning.COHERENCE_LOSS_CYCLE2_SUBSTRATE_PER_S` (`tuning.gd:298`), `Player.CAUSE_SUBSTRATE` (`player.gd:34`) and the cause and explanation strings exist, but nothing applies the drain (no reader of the constant outside `tests/unit/test_tuning.gd:114`). Endless's deepest layer is the only place where simply being somewhere costs reality; without it the depth-12 Substrate is easier than depth 6 relative to its Null (radius 24 m, 2.8 m/s).
**Fix:** in the run (or the Director's Pursuit tick, since it already knows Cycle and stratum), while `cycle >= 2` and the stratum is Substrate and the player is not in `NO_LOSS_STATES`, call `player.apply_coherence(-0.2 * dt, &"substrate")`. Test the cause reaches `run_ended(&"substrate", ...)`. List it as stubbed in the cp-10 note until then.

### S5. The ending's credits bury the corridor under the full MIT text and 60 engine components (ruling outside 16 §6)
01 §8 rolls the credits "over the corridor"; 16 §6 (`docs/design/16_release_and_steam.md:59-64`) lists the lines and says the MIT text goes "in a `LICENSES` submenu reachable from the title's settings". The M2.15 ruling (`CHANGELOG.md:136`) put the full notice and every engine component into the roll (`game/src/ui/credits.gd:22-45`). On the bench (`ending_credits.png`) a grey panel of licence boilerplate covers most of the window and the sunlit corridor, the one image in the game where the world is fully real; it reads as a legal screen, not an ending. (`open_items.md:80` already notes the contrast.)
**Fix:** the ending roll prints 16 §6's lines only (the AI line, `Godot Engine. MIT license.`, the typeface line and its copyright, the sound line, `The full license texts ship with the game in LICENSES.txt.`, `Thank you for looking.`), with no backing panel (04 §2: backing only where the world makes text unreadable). `Credits.entries()` keeps the full list for the LICENSES submenu and the Archive. Amend the CHANGELOG line 136 reading.

### S6. No simulated full Descent: M2's acceptance and the Coherence economy are unmeasured
M2 acceptance (`15_production_plan.md:87`) asks for "simulated full Descents reach the Threshold". The sims play one level at a time from a fresh start (`game/tests/sim/sim_run.gd` header: "plays one level per seed, profile and depth"), and the run-flow tests stop at depth 2 or jump to the ending (`tests/sim/test_run_flow.gd:68`, `tests/sim/test_ending_flow.gd:54`). So nobody has measured pillar 1 across a Descent: whether +20 per exit and the item pool cover noclip spend, contacts and Static from depth 1 to 6, how often a run arrives at the Substrate under 50 Coherence, and how that combines with the R14b numbers (depth-6 exits 6/12, 7/12, 9/12 from 100 Coherence, `open_items.md:83`). A Null that kills half the bots starting at 100 may kill nearly all who arrive at 40.
**Fix:** a chained sim (`sim_run.gd --descent`) that carries `RunState` (Coherence, items, drops) through depths 1 to 6 with the existing bot profiles, writing Coherence at each arrival and the cause of death; a gate test with a handful of seeds asserting that at least one profile reaches the Threshold. Feed the table to M3.4 with the Null lethality item.

### S7. The Flicker death line is false for most Flicker deaths (01 §6 rule 5)
`CAUSE_EXPLANATIONS[&"flicker"]` reads "It came out of a lit fixture while your light was on." (`strings.gd:260`). A group lunge lands on a player standing in the group's light whatever the flashlight is doing (`game/src/errors/flicker_lunge.gd:30` resolves without reading the flashlight); only the attached lunge needs the beam. The summary would teach a wrong counter ("turn my light off under a lit fixture and I am safe"). 00 §5: death explains itself.
**Fix:** "It came out of the lights you stood in." (true for both lunges; the attached lunge also starts in a group's light). If the summary should distinguish the attached lunge, add a second cause key rather than a second sentence.

### S8. `DISTANCE WALKED` is always 0
`RunState.distance_m` (`game/src/core/run_state.gd:32`) is written to `stats.distance_walked_m` at run end (`game_state.gd:324`) and printed by the Archive (`ARCHIVE_DISTANCE`), but nothing increments it (open item `open_items.md:32`). 13 §2 and 04 §7 list it; a statistic that never moves reads as broken.
**Fix:** `PlayerLocomotion` adds the horizontal distance of each grounded physics step to `GameState.run.distance_m` (not during noclip pass, drop or Landing). Test with a scripted walk.

### S9. The Feedback Contract record is stale and the Threshold row is still pending
`FeedbackRows` still marks "Threshold crossed" `PENDING` with "the ending scene lands with M2.15" (`game/src/debug/feedback_rows.gd:143`), although the ending, `threshold_tone` and the white cut exist. `docs/qa/feedback_checklist.md` is the M1.12 run (header line 3) and still lists closed gaps (lines 43, 46, 53: `[silence]` caption, Echo caption, unlock chime) and `pending` M2 rows (line 67). 11 §7 asks the milestone review to tick every row; the record cannot be ticked as it stands.
**Fix:** implement the `threshold` row (I: white cut layer alpha, S: `play.threshold_tone`, R: HUD hidden; the test can drive `Run._cross_threshold` without changing scene) and regenerate the checklist with the bench's `--md` output in the same change.

### S10. Settings that persist but do nothing: `auto_sprint`
`auto_sprint` (12 §7) is in the schema (`game/src/core/settings_schema.gd:109`) and the menu, with no consumer in `game/src` (the stamina lockout code in `player_locomotion.gd:229-235` never reads it). M2 acceptance says "every setting persists"; a toggle with no effect is worse than no toggle.
**Fix:** in `PlayerLocomotion`, after a lockout ends and stamina refills, resume Sprint only when `auto_sprint` is on and the sprint key is still held; default off keeps today's behaviour. Test both values.

### S11. `human_check_M2.md` asks the human for things that are not built
A1 and A4 expect the title's live corridor and its FOV change (`docs/qa/human_check_M2.md:47`, `:59`); the corridor is the M3 hook `Title.set_background` (`game/src/ui/title.gd:68-70`). E3 expects the 2 Hz pulse (S2). The human will log failures for deferred work and lose trust in the list.
**Fix:** mark A1/A4's corridor clauses "(M3: not yet)" and E3 "(fails until S2)", or remove them until the features land.

### S12. Every Landing is the elevator cabin, the Substrate's included
05 §4: "stratum-themed: elevator for Halls, Garage, Offices; stairwell landing for Pools, Server; a lit white pocket for Substrate" (open items `open_items.md:72`, `:76` item 7). The step from depth 5 into the unfinished floor passes through the same brushed-steel cabin as every other level, which is the one transition where the fiction's "below this floor we stopped" should start before the door opens.
**Fix (M3, before cp-12):** at least the Substrate pocket: the cabin shell drawn with the Substrate materials (lines on black, `u_floor` 0.55) and one studio light, the item panel unchanged. The stairwell landing can follow.
