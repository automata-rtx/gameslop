## Should-fix

### S1. The Coherence dial is pinned at 100 through depths 1 to 5, and the Landing prints a gain it does not give (pillar 1, the identity line)
Evidence: the table above (direct 100 on all 18 arrivals; cautious 98 to 100), and `docs/qa/descent_sim.md:39-40` (the M3.4 "after" rows, 20 Descents each: direct and cautious arrive at 93 to 100 at every depth; noclip spent 0 to 4.5 a run). `Tuning.COHERENCE_GAIN_PROPER_EXIT` 20 (`game/src/core/tuning.gd:272`) refills most of any contact (25 to 35), and the cap means a player who is not hit never sees the renderer move. The identity line ("how real the world looks is how alive you are", 00 §2, §6 "Players at 15 Coherence play in a near-monochrome, trembling world") is then shown only on depth 6 and during the seconds after a contact. Separately, the Landing panel always prints `COHERENCE +20` (`game/src/core/landing.gd:107` passes the constant; `game/src/ui/landing_panel.gd:82`), and at 100 the arrival applies nothing and plays no gain pulse (`game/src/player/player.gd:207-208` returns on a zero change): a printed promise that is false on most Landings (01 §6 rule 5; the run bench's `landing.png` shows `COHERENCE +20` at 100).
The bots do not noclip (the spend side is untested), so this is not yet a tuning verdict; it is the question cp-12 must answer.
**Fix:** (a) now, presentation only: the panel prints the gain it will apply, `min(20, 100 − coherence)` (`COHERENCE +7`), and no gain line at 100 (the Feedback row "Coherence gain" is unchanged: it fires whenever a gain applies). (b) Pre-agree the cp-12 decision so it is not argued afterwards: if the human runs arrive at depths 3 to 6 averaging ≥ 90 Coherence with fewer than 2 noclips a level, halve the proper-exit gain to 10 (05 §4, 06 §9, `tuning.gd` and `test_tuning.gd` together, CHANGELOG with the data); if they arrive below 70, leave it. (c) The sim bots hoard Polaroids (cautious ended every Descent with 1 to 3, the explorer died holding 3 at depth 5 with 1.5 Coherence for four minutes): their "use below 55" rule did not fire for the explorer (`tests/sim/sim_bot_items.gd`; test code), so the economy tables undercount recovery for low players. Fix the rule before the next sweep.

### S2. A lingering player's Build saturates: the sawtooth becomes a plateau with a scare every 30 s (pillar 3)
Evidence: the explorer telemetry above; `docs/qa/descent_sim.md:103` already saw it ("Build sits high for a lingering player … 0.89 to 0.95 for 1.5 to 7 minutes"). 10 §2 (`docs/design/10_director.md:24`) adds +0.10 per 0.5 s of cranking in every phase: a 4 s crank to full is +0.8, the same as 80 s of the time input, and Calm and Relief stop only the time and nearest-hunter rows (`10_director.md:43`). So a player who cranks at a level start leaves Calm above the 0.8 wake (R10 fires it once per Build, `CHANGELOG.md:98`), and if the woken hunter does not find them, nothing in 10 §2 brings intensity down: Build has no exit but a chase (`10_director.md:41`), and scares run at their cap (`SCARE_MIN_INTERVAL` 30 s, `tuning.gd:620`). Pillar 3 promises build, peak and relief; music and the threat vignette read intensity, so this is minutes of maximum dread, and 15 scares in eight minutes is not "rare, earned".
**Fix (orchestrator ruling, then numbers in 10 and `tuning.gd` together):** the crank row counts only in Build and Peak, like the time row (one line in `DirectorInputs._on_crank_tick` gated on the phase; 10 §2 table note). If cp-12's human runs (script Stage 4 and descent_sim item 6) still report a flat top, add: when Build has held ≥ 0.95 for 60 s with no chase, re-arm the existing 0.8 wake (wake or re-hint the nearest hunter to 12 m). That uses a behaviour the Director already has; it adds no error behaviour. Do not lower the scare cap without the human data.
