## 1. Name

## 2. Short description (300 characters at most) (16)

A first-person horror roguelite about falling out of reality. Descend through generated liminal floors, pass through walls at the cost of how real you are, and learn the single rule of each thing hunting you. Made, start to finish, by an AI.

## 3. Long description (new, from 16: premise, loop, honesty line)

NOCLIP is a first-person horror roguelite about falling out of reality. Each Descent drops you through a stack of generated places that were built to be passed through: yellow halls, drained pools, parking decks, dead offices, a server floor, and last the Substrate, where the world stops being drawn. Every level has one proper exit. Your only real tool is the noclip: hold the button and you pass through a wall, or through the floor, at the cost of your Coherence.

Coherence is how real you still are. It is your health, the price of every noclip, and the picture itself. The less you have, the less real the world looks: colour drains, the image grains and trembles, and at zero you dissolve. What hunts you are errors: faults in the rendering. Static, Still, Flicker, Echo and Null each have one rule and one counter, and none of them reaches you without a tell. Learn the rule. Then find yourself in a place where following it is hard.

Walk, listen, light the way, and decide: find the exit and descend safely, or noclip down now and arrive louder. Reach the Threshold at depth 6 and you win. Then go back for the score, the 36 notes, the Daily Descent and Endless.

Every system, line of code, shader, sound, and word in NOCLIP was designed and built by an AI. A human chose the project, published it, and did nothing else. That is the experiment.

## 5. Features (bullets) (16)

- 6 generated strata
- 5 errors, each with one rule
- Noclip through walls and floors
- Coherence renders the world
- 36 notes
- 14 unlocks and 4 loadouts
- Daily Descent
- Endless
- Full rebinding, FOV 70 to 110, a sensitivity slider, and accessibility options including sound captions

## 6. System requirements (16)

Minimum:

- OS: Windows 10 64-bit or Linux x86_64
- Processor: 4-core CPU
- Memory: 8 GB RAM
- Graphics: Vulkan 1.2 GPU with 2 GB VRAM (GTX 960 / RX 470 class)
- Storage: 500 MB available space

Recommended:

- Graphics: GTX 1060 / RX 580
- Memory: 16 GB RAM

Controller: not supported. NOCLIP is played with a keyboard and mouse only (16).

## 7. AI disclosure (Steam's AI generated content section) (new, from 16 §5 and §6)

Paste into the AI disclosure field:

NOCLIP was designed and built by an AI (Claude, Anthropic) using the Godot Engine. Every system, line of code, shader, sound, and word in the game, and this store page, was made by an AI. The game contains no imported meshes, no image textures and no recorded audio: the geometry is built from primitives, the surfaces are procedural, the interface glyphs are hand-written vector files, and the sound is synthesized from recipes. Nothing is generated by an AI while you play; the levels are made by ordinary seeded algorithms. A human chose the project and published it, and did nothing else.

## 8. Content descriptors (new, from 16 "content rating notes")

For Steam's content survey and the store page's mature content box:

- Violence: none shown. No gore, no blood, no corpses, no violence against depicted beings. The errors have no faces or bodies; contact costs Coherence and nothing else.
- Language: none. The only words in the world are 36 short notes.
- Sexual content and nudity: none.
- Substances: none.
- Themes: horror, dread, isolation, being lost. Jump scares are rare and are never how anything reaches you.
- Flashing lights: yes. Some effects flash (the Flicker lunge, contact, the Polaroid). The Reduce flashing option replaces them with a 200 ms soft fade and turns off the title screen's flicker. If the survey allows free text, say that.

## 13. Screenshots (16: eight from the tour)

Steam wants at least five at 1920 x 1080 or larger, in the 16:9 ratio. Capture all eight at 1920 x 1080. The CPU renderer runs at a few frames per second at this size, so expect minutes per frame; the pictures are the same as on a GPU apart from the render time. On a machine with a GPU, drop `tools/ci/render.sh` and run the same Godot arguments with `$GODOT_BIN` directly.

Run these three commands from the repository root. The tour writes `<out>/<stratum>/<pose>_c<coherence>.png`, seed 1.

```
tools/ci/render.sh --path game --resolution 1920x1080 -- --tour build/store --tour-only poses,noclip
tools/ci/render.sh --path game --resolution 1920x1080 res://scenes/debug/error_arena.tscn -- --shots build/store/garage --stratum garage
tools/ci/render.sh --path game --resolution 1920x1080 res://scenes/debug/error_arena.tscn -- --shots build/store/offices --stratum offices
```
