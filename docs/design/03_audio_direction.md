# 03 — Audio Direction

**Depends on:** `00_OVERVIEW.md`, `01_fiction_and_tone.md`, `02_visual_direction.md`
**Skills to read:** `godot-audio-systems`
**Pulls engagement levers:** readable rules (every error is heard before it is seen), dread over startle, tactile everything.

---

## 1. The audio thesis

Sound is the primary threat channel. The player hears Static before seeing the distortion, hears their own late footsteps before Echo is within reach, hears a room's lights stutter before Flicker lunges, and hears the grid hum of Null through walls. Picture is confirmation; sound is warning.

The second job of audio is weight. Every input has a sound within 50 ms (Feedback Contract, `11`). The third job is place: each stratum has a distinct room tone and reverb so the player knows where they are with their eyes closed.

The aesthetic is **electrical and architectural**: hums, fans, fluorescent ballasts, water on tile, carpet footsteps, relay clunks, and, for the unreal layer, synthesized noise bands, digital tearing, and sine clusters. No orchestral music. No screams. No growls. Nothing organic ever makes a sound except the player.

## 2. Sourcing pipeline (how an AI ships a sound library)

All sounds are produced by a deterministic synthesis script committed to the repo: `tools/audio/synth.py` (Python 3, numpy only; `scipy` optional for filters, with a numpy fallback). Running `python tools/audio/synth.py --out game/assets/audio` regenerates every WAV from the recipe table in `tools/audio/recipes.json`. Each recipe is a small graph: oscillators (sine, saw, square, noise white/pink/brown), envelopes (ADSR, exponential), filters (biquad low/high/band, one-pole), effects (saturation, bit-crush, simple comb reverb, delay), mixers, and a final normaliser to a target peak and LUFS-ish RMS.

Rules:
- 48 kHz, 16-bit, mono for 3D sounds, stereo for UI, music, and room tones.
- Every one-shot has 3 to 5 variations generated by seed, so repeats never sound identical.
- File naming: `category_name_vNN.wav` (e.g. `foot_carpet_v03.wav`). Godot imports with loop off except room tones and music, which are generated with seamless loop tails.
- CC0 recordings (Kenney, freesound CC0 only) are allowed where synthesis is clearly inferior (water lapping, a real relay clunk). Any imported file is listed in `CREDITS.md` with source and license. Prefer synthesis; the aesthetic suits it.
- Runtime `AudioStreamGenerator` is used for exactly two continuous layers that must react per frame: the Coherence static bed and the Null grid tone. Everything else is sample playback.

## 3. Buses and mix

```
Master
├── World          (3D, reverb send per stratum)
│   ├── Footsteps
│   ├── Interact
│   ├── Errors
│   └── Ambience   (room tone, fixtures, fans, water)
├── Player         (non-spatial: breath, heartbeat, crank, noclip, coherence bed)
├── Music          (generative drones)
└── UI
```

- Settings sliders (`12`): Master, World (labelled "Effects"), Ambience, Music, UI. Player bus follows Effects.
- **Ducking:** Errors bus ducks Ambience by 4 dB when any error is within 10 m. Noclip commit ducks everything but Player by 8 dB for 300 ms. Note pickup ducks Music by 6 dB for the sheet's duration.
- **Reverb:** one `AudioEffectReverb` on World, parameters swapped per stratum on level load with a 1 s crossfade: Halls (room 0.5, damping 0.6, wet 0.2), Pools (room 0.9, damping 0.2, wet 0.45, predelay 40 ms), Garage (room 0.8, damping 0.4, wet 0.3), Offices (room 0.4, damping 0.7, wet 0.15), Server (room 0.3, damping 0.8, wet 0.1), Substrate (room 1.0, damping 0.0, wet 0.5, with a high-pass at 300 Hz so it sounds like an empty render).
- **Limiter** on Master, ceiling -1 dBFS. **Low-shelf +2 dB at 90 Hz** on Player for weight.
- **Occlusion:** every 3D emitter in Errors and Interact is checked each 0.2 s with a ray to the listener; occluded emitters get a low-pass at 800 Hz and -6 dB (tween 100 ms). Static ignores occlusion (it is heard through walls by design); Null is heard through walls by design.
- **Attenuation:** inverse distance, `max_distance` per table below, `unit_size` 1.0, Doppler off.

## 5. Music

There is no composed music. There is a **generative drone system** (`MusicDirector`, in `game/src/audio/`), running on the Music bus:

- Per stratum, a 3-voice chord palette (Halls: A2, E3, B3; Pools: D2, A2, F3; Garage: G1, D2, Bb2; Offices: E2, B2, G3; Server: C2, G2, Eb3; Substrate: A1 only, with its 5th appearing when the Threshold is in view) rendered as long synthesized pads (sine plus detuned saw at -18 dB, LP 600 Hz, 12 s loops with 2 s crossfades, three variations each).
- Voices fade in and out on a slow random schedule (one change every 20 to 45 s) so the drone never loops audibly.
- **Intensity coupling** (`10`): at Director intensity above 0.6 the LP opens to 1.8 kHz and a fourth voice a minor second above the root fades in at -10 dB. At relief, the fourth voice drops out first. At peak (chase), music drops out entirely for 4 s then returns with the fourth voice only. Silence is the loudest cue.
- **Title screen:** the Halls drone with a 0.2 Hz slow tremolo.
- **Ending corridor:** a single major triad in A (A3, C#4, E4) synthesized clean, 40 s, the only consonant chord in the game.

## 6. Mix rules

1. The player's own footsteps are always audible at -14 dB RMS peak at walk, because Echo's trick depends on the player knowing their own sound.
2. Nothing in the Errors bus exceeds -6 dBFS before the limiter. Loudness is not the scare. Contrast is.
3. Room tone never drops below -40 dB except during Still's silence (-46 dB) and Flicker's lunge (-inf for 1.5 s).
4. Every sample has a 2 ms fade-in to prevent clicks; loops are generated with crossfaded tails.
5. All one-shots play through `AudioStreamRandomizer` with pitch ±4% and the variation set.
6. Sound cue captions (`12`): every Errors bus event and every door/breaker event emits `EventBus.audio_cue(text, position)` for the caption system, with the text in the table in `04` §10.

## 7. Verification

- `tools/audio/synth.py --verify` renders every recipe and asserts peak and RMS targets, loop seams (first/last 10 ms correlation), and no DC offset.
- An in-game audio test scene (`game/scenes/debug/audio_board.tscn`) lists every sound with a play button, used by the human and by the agent audio pass.

## Interfaces

- `AudioManager` autoload: `play_3d(id: StringName, pos: Vector3, bus := &"Errors")`, `play_2d(id: StringName)`, `start_loop(id, node) -> AudioStreamPlayer3D`, `set_stratum(stratum: StringName)` (swaps reverb and room tone), `duck(bus, db, seconds)`.
- `EventBus.noise_emitted(pos: Vector3, radius: float, kind: StringName)` (defined in `06`) is consumed by errors; `AudioManager` also listens to it to play the matching sample, so a noise and its sound are the same event.
- `EventBus.audio_cue(text: String, pos: Vector3)` for captions.
- `MusicDirector`: `set_intensity(v: float)`, `set_stratum(s)`, `silence(seconds)`.

### Interface additions during production
- `game/assets/audio/manifest.json` is the sample catalogue `AudioManager` loads: id → files, bus, loop, channels, `runtime` pitch range. Its ids are the ids `play_2d`/`play_3d` take. One-shot variations are wrapped in `AudioStreamRandomizer` with ±4% pitch (§6 rule 5).
- `crank_loop` (ratchet) and `crank_whine` are separate loops so the whine pitch follows charge independently.
